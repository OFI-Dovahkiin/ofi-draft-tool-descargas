Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

OFI Draft Tool is a free Windows app and this website. Both are made by an independent developer based in Spain, who is responsible for the data described here. If anything is unclear, write to contact@ofidraft.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no analytics, in the app or on the website.
  • What the app reads from your League client and from the game stays on your PC.
  • The statistics we publish are aggregated. The exception is the profiles of the OFI team's own players, which ship inside the app (see below).

This website

The website is a set of pages and a small server, both hosted by Cloudflare. It has no analytics, loads no third-party scripts or fonts and sets no cookies; the only ones are the sign-in cookies of the team area (CF_Authorization and CF_AppSession, from Cloudflare Access), and only for those who sign in. Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. If something fails on the server, Cloudflare keeps its error messages for a few days so we can fix it; we do not write what you send or your IP address into them.

The form for bugs, ideas and misread drafts sends the developer an email with what you write, any file you attach, your browser's user agent and the country Cloudflare places your connection in. It is not stored on the website. To stop mass sending, the server remembers for 10 minutes how many reports came from your connection, keyed by a hash of your IP address rather than the address itself. Email sent to contact@ofidraft.com is forwarded by Cloudflare to the developer's mailbox.

Downloading the installer requires the team password. The server checks it and does not store it. To stop password guessing, it remembers for 10 minutes how many attempts came from your connection, as with the form: keyed by a hash of your IP address rather than the address itself. The server fetches the installer from GitHub, where it is stored.

The app on your PC

The app connects to:

  • The League client on your own PC, through its local API. It reads the champion select session (champions, bans, positions and the names your client shows for your own team), the game phase, the lobby (each member's Riot ID and preferred position), your own summoner and the list of queues. To notice changes it listens to the client's events, which also carry other things (chat, friends…): it discards those as they arrive, without storing them. When you press Import, it writes your summoner spells, an item set and a rune page of its own, whose name starts with «OFI ». If you already have a page whose name starts like that, it reuses it and overwrites it; if not, it creates one and first saves a copy of your active page on your PC.
  • The game on your own PC, through Riot's local Live Client Data API, and only if you turn the overlay on: your champion, level, gold, items and abilities; the side, position and items of the other nine players, which it turns at once into a gold figure; and the epic objective events.
  • Riot's Data Dragon (ddragon.leagueoflegends.com), to load the images of champions, items, runes and abilities. Riot sees your IP address, as with any image on the web.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), to load the app's typefaces. Google sees your IP address; see Google's privacy policy.
  • GitHub, to check for new versions and download them. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download. See GitHub's privacy statement.

What the app reads from the client and the game never leaves your PC. The app keeps these files in your user folder, never uploads them, and you can delete them at any time:

  • Your settings, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\OFI Draft Clash Tool.
  • A session diary of what it recommended, so you can review it, and an error log, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\OFI Draft Gap Tool V2.
  • In %APPDATA%\com.ofi.draftgaptool: the line-up chosen in the Team tab, the list of players the app follows, Clash scouting (deleted after 7 days), what was downloaded for the substitutes added (it stays even after «Quitarlos», until you delete it), a copy of your active rune page each time runes go into a new page (the last 10) and a note of the last update attempt.

Data processed with the Riot API

  • Ranked games. A scheduled job on our build server downloads public ranked Solo/Duo games from the EUW server through the official Riot Games API and aggregates them by champion and role. The downloaded games stay in our private build environment for as long as they are needed for the statistics. Only aggregates, with no player-level data, are published in the app.
  • Team profiles. For the players on the OFI team's list, and only for them, a weekly job stores their Riot ID, their PUUID (Riot's player identifier), their rank, their champion mastery and all their games in Normal Draft, Ranked Solo/Duo, Ranked Flex and Clash since September 2024, which the app shows in its Players, Team and History tabs. The app also carries a career summary of each of them taken from op.gg by its first version, which we are removing. These profiles ship inside the app, and the app can be downloaded from this website. Before a public release, each team's data will only reach that team. A player can ask to be removed by writing to contact@ofidraft.com: from the next weekly version the profile is no longer in the app, and we take down the earlier versions that carried it. Copies already installed lose it when they update.
  • Clash scouting in the team area. The team area of this website can do the same Clash scouting, with the developer's Riot API key kept on the server: see The team area.
  • Captain tools. Two tools, Clash scouting and adding a substitute, run on the team captain's PC with a Riot API key set on that PC. They read, for the Riot IDs the captain types, the player's rank, champion mastery and up to 20 public games per queue in four queues. Scouting is used to prepare that match, is never published and is deleted after 7 days. A substitute's data stays only on that PC, for the Clash, and does not put them on the team's list; «Quitarlos» (remove them) empties everything downloaded about them. Someone joins the team's list, and from the next weekly update has their data handled like any other player's (above), only if the captain adds their Riot ID to it on purpose.

We use this data because it is necessary for what the app does: showing statistics from public game data and helping a team prepare its matches. We do not sell or share it, and we do not use it for advertising.

The team area

The team area (ofidraft.com/equipo) is only for the players of the OFI team. You sign in with Cloudflare Access: you type your email address and receive a code. Cloudflare keeps a log of each sign-in (email address, date, IP address and country) to protect the area; see Cloudflare's privacy policy, linked above.

What the team area stores, at Cloudflare:

  • Your profile: your email address (to know it is you; only the captains see it), the nickname, Riot ID and position you enter, and when you last visited. The team sees your nickname, Riot ID and position. Each member types their own Riot ID: the team area does not check that it is theirs.
  • What you write: ideas, drafts, builds, matchup notes, Clash plans, reviews and comments, with your nickname and the date, and your votes. The team sees them. They stay until you or a captain delete them. What a captain exports for the app (accepted builds, matchup notes that have not been withdrawn, misread drafts) carries neither your nickname nor your email address.
  • Clash scouting: for the Riot IDs of opponents that someone looks up, what the Riot API returns: their Clash team and position, their rank, their champion mastery and, from their last games (up to 10, Solo/Duo and Clash), the champion, position, result and KDA. It is used to prepare that Clash, never leaves the team area and is deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Reviews: if you bring in the Clash or Flex games of a day, for each game it keeps the ten champions with their positions, the side, the result and the length, with no names. A review can include a link to a video.
  • The automatic review sheet: when someone asks for it, what Riot's data says about that game (lanes at 10 and 15 minutes, deaths, teamfights, objectives and clip candidates), with champions and positions only, no names; and, to find it, the game number and the PUUID of whoever brought it in. It is kept for 30 days, and deleted earlier if that person is removed from the area.
  • The builds you actually play: for members who entered their Riot ID, when someone asks for it, from their last games (up to 12, Solo/Duo, Flex and Clash): the champion, position, result, runes, summoner spells and final items; and, to find them, their PUUID (Riot's player identifier) and the list of those games. Kept for 30 days, and deleted sooner if they change or remove their Riot ID, or if they are deleted from the team area.

The team area's pages load the names and icons of champions, items, runes and summoner spells from Riot's Data Dragon (Riot sees your IP address). If you install the team area (on your phone or your computer), that browser keeps a copy of its pages, your profile and the Clash plans, so they open without a connection, and also uses it when you open the team area in a normal tab of that browser; «Salir» (sign out) deletes it. If you do not install it, nothing is kept.

To have your data deleted (your profile, what you wrote and what was fetched from your games), write to contact@ofidraft.com.

Who else handles data

GitHub runs our build server and hosts the downloads, Cloudflare hosts this website and the team area and forwards its email, and Google serves the app's fonts. All three are based in the United States and process data under their own privacy policies, linked above, which describe the safeguards they apply to transfers from the European Union.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its use, by writing to contact@ofidraft.com. We answer within one month. If you think we have not handled your data well, you can complain to the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos or to the data protection authority of your country.

Children

The app does not ask for or collect personal data from you. If you are under 14, ask a parent or guardian before using it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
