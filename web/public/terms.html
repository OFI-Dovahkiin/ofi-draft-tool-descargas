What OFI Draft Tool is

A free draft assistant for League of Legends. It analyses champion select and shows statistics and explanations built from public game data. It is made by an independent developer and is not affiliated with Riot Games.

Using the app

  • You may install and use the app for free, for your own play and your team's.
  • Do not sell it, redistribute modified copies, or remove the notices it contains.
  • You must also follow Riot Games' Terms of Service while you play. The app is built to respect Riot's developer policies: it does not read the game's memory, does not act for you, and its optional overlay only uses the game's official live data.

The information it shows

Statistics and recommendations are estimates from past games and from our champion database. They can be wrong or out of date, especially right after a patch. The app gives options; every pick, ban and build remains your decision.

Updates and availability

The app updates itself with versions signed by us. It installs one on its own only when it starts and the League client is closed, on its home screen, in the lobby or on the end-of-game screen; at any other time it waits until you press Update. We may change features or stop the service at any time. If Riot Games limits our access to its API, the statistics stop updating.
