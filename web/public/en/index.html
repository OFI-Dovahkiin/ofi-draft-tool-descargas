Why your draft wins, and how hard it is to play.

OFI Draft Tool reads your champion select from the League client and explains each composition in plain words: the lanes, the power curve, the game plan and what the other team has to do to stop it. A free Windows app, made by and for the OFI Clash team.

See what it does Read the fair-play rules

What it shows in champion select

  • Both teams, as the client shows them. Picks, bans and your team's hovers. The enemy's roles are not sent by the client, so the app infers them from the visible champions, says so on screen and lets you correct them.
  • Lane by lane. Which side is favoured in each lane according to the matchup, with gold at 15 minutes and the win rate next to it as a check, each with its sample size.
  • The game plan of each team. When each composition is strongest, how it wants to fight (engage, peel, poke, split) and what the other side needs to deny it.
  • Options for the next pick and ban. Several candidates, ranked, each with the reason behind it. The decision stays with the player.
  • Runes, summoner spells and an item set for your champion, written to the client only when you press the import button. Runes go into the app's own page, whose name starts with «OFI »: if you already have a page named like that, it reuses and overwrites it; if not, it creates one and first saves a copy of your active page on your PC.

Outside champion select it works as a reference: a tier list by role, a page per champion with builds, rune pages, matchups and win rate by rank, the team's players and match history, and a manual board where you can type ten champions to study a composition.

How you use it

  1. Install it.A Windows 10 or 11 installer that does not need administrator rights. It updates itself with versions signed by us: on its own only when it starts and the client is closed, on its home screen, in the lobby or on the end-of-game screen; otherwise it waits until you press Update.
  2. Open the League client.The app finds it on its own by reading the client's lockfile. It does not touch the game process.
  3. Enter champion select.The analysis refreshes with every pick, ban and hover from your team. The board sits in its own window next to the client.
  4. Lock in, and import if you want to.Press Import to write the suggested runes, spells and item set to the client.

During the game, only if you turn it on

The overlay is optional and off by default. It is a small transparent window on top of the game, fed only by Riot's Live Client Data API, the local interface the game itself offers. It never reads the game's memory.

  • The game plan from your draft: the style of your composition and how it wins.
  • Which ability to level next, from the skill orders measured in ranked games: the share of games that level it at that point, and how many games.
  • The next item of your build that you do not have yet, and the gold you are missing for it, counting the components you already have.
  • Epic objective timers: from the patch schedule, or from the game's own events once that objective has died in the game (scheduled times carry a «~»). A reminder at 1:30, 1:00 and 0:30 says which objective and when. It never says what to do.
  • Gold in items: two differences, your team against theirs and you against your lane opponent, from the items the scoreboard already shows. From the next version they count each item at its full shop price and show your own unspent gold next to them.

It never shows enemy cooldowns, enemy summoner spells, positions or jungle camps.

For the team

  • Players. Each member's roles, champion pool and recent form, measured on their own games.
  • Game plans. Compositions the five of you can actually play, with who takes each role, who comes in if a champion is banned, and which bans protect the plan.
  • Match history. The team's recent games, with filters by queue, result, role and champion.
  • Clash scouting. Before a Clash match, the champions the opposing players play, from their public games.

Every statistic comes with its sample size. When there is not enough data, the app says so instead of filling the gap.

Fair play

The tool is built for the time before the game. These rules are part of the code, not a promise added afterwards.

What it does

  • Talks to the client and the game only through Riot's official interfaces: the League client's local API and the Live Client Data API.
  • Works in champion select and, if you turn the overlay on, during the game.
  • Offers several options with their reasons; the player decides.
  • Writes runes, spells and items only when you press a button.
  • Shows the source and the sample size of every statistic.

What it never does

  • Read or modify the game's memory, inject code or hook into the game.
  • Show enemy cooldowns, enemy summoner spells, positions or jungle camps.
  • Reveal hidden players: enemy names in champion select stay hidden; the app does not even read them.
  • Act for you: no automatic picks, bans, purchases or level-ups.
  • Rate players with an MMR or elo estimate.

Status

Made for the OFI team, and used by it. The installer can be downloaded from the team's site, but the app is not promoted outside the team; a public release will come after Riot reviews and approves the product. The app is updated with fresh data every week.

The team's own site, in Spanish, has the download, every feature in detail and a form to report problems: ofidraft.com. If you are reviewing OFI Draft Tool for Riot Games, the test instructions are in our application.
